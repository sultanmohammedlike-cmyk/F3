ABOUT F3

Born in the vibrant coastal city of Mangalore, F3 is a GenZ-focused clothing brand that blends streetwear aesthetics with classy sophistication. We believe fashion is an expression of individuality and creativity.


Our collections are curated for the bold, the expressive, and the trendsetters who aren't afraid to stand out. Each piece is designed with attention to detail, quality fabrics, and a touch of urban edge that defines the GenZ spirit.


At F3, we're not just selling clothes—we're promoting a culture of self-expression, confidence, and style that transcends boundaries.
